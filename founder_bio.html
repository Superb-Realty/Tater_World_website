Social Impact

Initiatives That Matter

Beyond formal roles, Shri Khyalilal Tater has been deeply committed to social service through philanthropic initiatives spanning education, healthcare, and community development.

Education & Empowerment
Believing that education is the foundation of a stronger society, he has supported numerous initiatives that empower young minds.
  • Established Jivisha School, Mankhurd — for education and care of mentally challenged girls
  • Scholarships, educational loans and financial assistance for meritorious students
  • Support for underprivileged students seeking higher education
Healthcare Initiatives
Healthcare accessibility has been a key focus area of his philanthropic work, with multiple initiatives across Mumbai.
  • Established a dialysis centre at Rajawadi Hospital, Mumbai
  • Donation of ambulances for emergency medical response
  • Organised eye check-up camps for students and community members
  • Extended medical assistance to economically disadvantaged individuals
Community Development
Strengthening community infrastructure and promoting spiritual and cultural unity across the Jain community.
  • Construction of Terapanth Bhawan, Kandivali — a spiritual, cultural and social hub
  • Support for Terapanth community initiatives encouraging collective growth
  • Active participation in building community bonds across Mumbai and Rajasthan
Knowledge & Leadership
Through JITO, enabling the next generation of Jain entrepreneurs and professionals through mentorship and skill development.
  • Mentorship programs for young Jain professionals
  • Business collaboration initiatives through JITO networks
  • Youth development and leadership training programs
  • Knowledge-sharing and philanthropic activities
